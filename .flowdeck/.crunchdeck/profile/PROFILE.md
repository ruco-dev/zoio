# Profile: Zoio

**Status:** MVP in development

## Overview

> Local-first AI search visibility scanner and query discovery engine.

---

## One-liner

Zoio is a local CLI that shows which businesses appear in AI answers and discovers the queries around them.

## Elevator Pitch

Zoio helps marketers, local businesses, and SEO practitioners understand visibility in AI-generated recommendations without creating an account or sending results to a dashboard. It scans location-and-intent queries, extracts recommended entities and citations, and saves a portable local dataset. Fixture, Codex, and optional direct OpenAI modes keep testing deterministic while supporting live investigation.

## Success Signal

**North star:** Number of completed, locally stored AI-visibility scans that lead to an actionable query or entity insight per month.

## Core Features

- Scan AI answers for entities, citations, and target visibility.
- Persist portable JSONL evidence locally.
- Explore related query space within explicit depth and count limits.

## Market Lane & Differentiation

| Competitor / Category | What they do | How Zoio differs |
|---|---|---|
| Traditional SEO rank trackers | Track rankings in search engine result pages | Measures entity presence in AI-generated answers. |
| Enterprise AI visibility platforms | Provide hosted monitoring and dashboards | Is local-first, open source, private by default, and scriptable as a CLI. |

**The edge in one line:** Reproducible AI visibility evidence without an account, backend, or dashboard.
